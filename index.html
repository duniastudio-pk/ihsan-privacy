1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application Ihsan: Prayer Time & Quran ("Application"), provided by DUNIA Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). This document informs you of our policies regarding the collection, processing, and disclosure of data when you use our Application.

By installing and using Ihsan, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Location Data Usage & Disclosures (Google Play Compliant)

In strict adherence to Google Play's Location Permissions Policy, we disclose the exact manner in which location data is handled:

  • Purpose: Ihsan accesses your device's geographical coordinates (latitude, longitude, and elevation) solely to calculate astronomical prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), sunrise/sunset times, and the accurate direction of the Qibla (Kaaba compass).
  • On-Device Ephemeral Processing: All astronomical equations and compass headings are calculated strictly on-device. Your coordinates are processed ephemerally in memory.
  • No Tracking or Cloud Uploads: Ihsan never logs, tracks, transmits, or stores your location history on remote servers. Your location is never shared with third parties or advertisers.
  • Manual Selection Alternative: Granting GPS permission is completely optional. Users may select their city manually from an offline database without granting device location permissions.

The table below provides clear technical justification for every permission utilized by Ihsan:

ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Location Used strictly on-device to compute high-precision prayer times and Qibla compass bearing. Never uploaded or shared.

4. On-Device Data Storage

All user preferences and personal religious tracking data are kept strictly private on your device:

  • Quran Progress & Bookmarks: Your reading history, last read Ayah, and custom surah bookmarks are stored exclusively in your local SQLite/Room database.
  • Tasbih & Dhikr Counters: Your personalized digital counter history, daily goals, and habit streaks remain on your device.
  • Audio Cache: Downloaded recitations are saved in your app's private cache for offline listening and can be deleted at any time through the app settings.

Google AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID) and diagnostic telemetry to serve compliant ads according to Google Play Policies.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

  • Zero Server Data: Because DUNIA Studio does not transmit or store your personal information on cloud servers, there is no remote user data to retain or delete.
  • Full User Control: You can reset all counters, clear audio downloads, or erase reading history directly from the application settings.
  • App Removal: Completely uninstalling the Application instantly erases all local database records, preferences, and cached audio from your device.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Ihsan is suitable for general audiences of all ages, including families. The Application does not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
