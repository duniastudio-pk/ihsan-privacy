Privacy Policy

App: Ihsan: Prayer Time & Quran
Developer: DUNIA Studio
Effective Date: September 12, 2026

At DUNIA Studio, we are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application, Ihsan: Prayer Time & Quran, handles user data.

1. Information Access & Usage

  • Location Data (Precise and Approximate): Ihsan accesses your device location solely to calculate accurate local prayer times, sunrise/sunset times, and the Qibla compass direction. Location data is processed ephemerally on-device and is never transmitted to external servers, sold, or shared with third parties. Users may also choose to set location manually without granting GPS permission.
  • Local On-Device Storage: Your personal reading bookmarks, Quran reading progress, Tasbih counters, and habit streaks are stored strictly on your local device. We do not maintain any cloud-based user databases.
  • Audio Playback: When streaming or downloading Quran recitations, audio requests are made directly to standard public recitation CDNs. No personal information is associated with these requests.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Services

Ihsan may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable device identifiers to serve ads in compliance with Google Play Developer policies. For more information on how Google handles advertising data, visit:

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Ihsan does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to remote servers, you retain full ownership and control of your information. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored preferences from your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Ihsan is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
